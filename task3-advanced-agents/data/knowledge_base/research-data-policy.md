# Research Data Handling Policy (effective 1 January 2026)

## Scope
Applies to all Synnovate staff and contractors handling customer or third-party data
for research, model training, or product evaluation.

## Data classification
- **Green** — fully synthetic or public data. No restrictions.
- **Amber** — customer data that has been anonymised and aggregated. Internal use only.
- **Red** — identifiable customer data. Requires named approval before any use.

## Retention periods
Amber research datasets must be deleted within 180 days of the project closing.
Red datasets must be deleted within 30 days of the approved purpose being completed.

## Anonymisation requirements
Removal of direct identifiers alone is not sufficient. Datasets must pass k-anonymity
with k of at least 5 across all quasi-identifier combinations before reclassification
from Red to Amber.

## Approval workflow
Red data access requires sign-off from both the Data Protection Officer and the
customer's named account owner. Approvals expire after 90 days and are not renewable
without a fresh review.
